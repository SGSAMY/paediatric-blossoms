Meet Dr Sathish Krishnan

Dr Krishnan has almost 20 years of experience caring for babies, children and teenagers, including more than nine years as an NHS consultant. He sees children with both common and more specialist conditions, and takes the time to make sure families understand what is happening and why.

Specialist heart care for children

Many children are referred for a heart murmur, palpitations or fainting. Dr Krishnan’s assessment includes an ECG and, where needed, an echocardiogram: a painless ultrasound scan of the heart. Families leave with clear answers and a plan.

Arrange a heart assessment
  • Heart murmursECG and echocardiography to check for congenital and acquired heart conditions.
  • PalpitationsECG, blood tests, echocardiography and ambulatory (24-hour) ECG.
  • Fainting, vasovagal syncope and POTSLying and standing blood pressure, ECG, bloods and echo to rule out other causes, tilt-test referral if needed, and ongoing management.
  • Family heart conditionsScreening where conditions such as cardiomyopathy run in the family.
  • Connective tissue disordersChecking the heart in conditions such as Marfan’s syndrome.

Privacy notice

This notice explains how Paediatric Blossoms uses the information you send us through the booking form on this website.

Who we are

Paediatric Blossoms is the private paediatric practice of Dr Sathish Krishnan, part of Evergreen Blossoms Ltd. We are the data controller for the information you give us. You can contact us at paediatricblossoms@gmail.com or on 07733 907976.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • How you will pay, and your insurer, membership number and pre-authorisation code if you use insurance
  • Your child’s name, date of birth and home address
  • Your child’s GP and surgery
  • What you would like help with, which may include information about your child’s health

Why we use it

We use these details to contact you, arrange your child’s appointment and prepare for the consultation. Information about your child’s health is special category data, and we only use it to provide healthcare.

Under UK data protection law, our lawful bases are taking steps at your request before providing care (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR) and the provision of health care (Article 9(2)(h) UK GDPR).

Who we share it with

  • Netlify, which hosts this website, stores booking requests and sends them to us by email. Netlify is based in the United States, so your details may be processed outside the UK, with the safeguards UK law requires.
  • Google, which provides the practice’s email service.
  • Your insurer, if you choose to pay through insurance.
  • Your child’s GP and other healthcare professionals involved in their care, where this is part of their treatment.

We never sell your information or use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Booking requests are deleted from the website system once an appointment has been arranged, and in any case within [3 months]. If your child becomes a patient, their medical records are kept for the periods recommended for children’s health records in the UK.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. To do any of these, email us at the address above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Urgent medical help

The booking form is not monitored around the clock. If your child is seriously unwell, call 999 or go to your nearest A&E. For urgent advice, call NHS 111.
