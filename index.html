Meet Dr Sathish Krishnan

Dr Krishnan has almost 20 years of experience caring for babies, children and teenagers, including more than nine years as an NHS consultant. He sees children with both common and more specialist conditions, and takes the time to make sure families understand what is happening and why.

His particular interest is the heart. He provides dedicated neonatal and paediatric echocardiography (heart scan) services, alongside his general paediatric practice.

Specialist heart care for children

Many children are referred for a heart murmur, palpitations or fainting. Dr Krishnan’s assessment includes an ECG and, where needed, an echocardiogram: a painless ultrasound scan of the heart. Families leave with clear answers and a plan.

Arrange a heart assessment
  • Heart murmursECG and echocardiography to check for congenital and acquired heart conditions.
  • PalpitationsECG, blood tests, echocardiography and ambulatory (24-hour) ECG.
  • Fainting, vasovagal syncope and POTSLying and standing blood pressure, ECG, bloods and echo to rule out other causes, tilt-test referral if needed, and ongoing management.
  • Family heart conditionsScreening where conditions such as cardiomyopathy run in the family.
  • Connective tissue disordersChecking the heart in conditions such as Marfan’s syndrome.

Conditions we help with

From newborn feeding worries to teenage headaches. Every consultation includes a careful history and examination, with tests only when they are genuinely needed.

Babies

Reflux, colic, milk intolerance, food allergy, eczema, faltering growth, head shape and FPIES.

Breathing

Asthma, chronic cough, recurrent chest infections, hay fever and allergy.

Development

General developmental assessment and investigation of delayed development.

Brain & nerves

Seizures, headaches, funny turns and tics, with BP checks and MRI if needed.

Tummy & bowel

Constipation, diarrhoea, abdominal pain, vomiting, ulcers and coeliac disease.

Bladder & kidneys

Bedwetting, urgency, repeated urine infections, antenatal kidney dilatation and blood in the urine.

Skin

Eczema, urticaria (hives) and childhood rashes.

Joints

Joint pain, arthritis and hypermobility.

Growth & hormones

Thyroid conditions, short stature and childhood obesity.

Sleep

Sleep problems in babies, children and teenagers.

Heart

Murmurs, palpitations, fainting and family heart conditions. See heart care
